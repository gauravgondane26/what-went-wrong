Per goal (`collapse-baseline.json` vs `collapse-after.json`):
- 15 goals changed.
- 13 had no collapse before and get one now.
- 2 moved to a different frame (one later, one earlier).
- None lost their collapse.

**How the rule was chosen.** 36 variants were simulated on the cached sequences:
- Shape threshold: 0.03, 0.05 or 0.08.
- Marking rise: 1, 1.5 or 2.
- Window: 1 or 2 frames.
- With or without requiring the ball within 36 units of goal.

What the variants showed:
- The marking rise mattered most: ≥ 1 took the no-collapse goals from 22 to 8–9, while ≥ 1.5 only got them to 13–15.
- The ball-distance condition never changed a result, so it was dropped.
- A 2-frame window costs one extra no-collapse goal but ignores one-frame camera blips, so it was preferred.
- Every marking collapse lands in the last 0–4 frames of its move, at the moment runners come free (typically 0, 0 → 2, 2 unmarked).

**Still open.**
- About half of crossed goals still collapse before the cross, because a real earlier shape drop takes priority.
- 5 corners and 3 free kicks show neither a drop nor a marking rise.
