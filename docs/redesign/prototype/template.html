      How to read the pitch

      The defending team always defends the goal on the left, so every goal reads the same way. Blue is the defence. Red marks the moment its shape broke.

      The shape score

      Each moment gets a score out of 100 built from three measures: how tight the defenders around the ball are (40%), how many are goal-side of the ball (30%), and how many passing lanes they cut off (30%). 70 and above is solid, 40 to 69 is stretched, under 40 is broken.

      The collapse

      The collapse is the biggest fall in the score across the move, comparing the two moments after each step with the two before it. Some goals have none: the shape held, or the camera showed too few defenders to judge.

      What the camera can't see

      Positions come from the broadcast picture, so players outside the frame are missing. When fewer than 6 defenders are in view, the moment isn't scored.
